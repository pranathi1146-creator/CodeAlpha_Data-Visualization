📘 WHAT IS THIS VISUALIZATION? - INFORMATIVE

This project visualizes Iris Flower Dataset - 150 flowers, but we use 30 moderate samples (10 each species) to show clear separation. Each flower has 4 measurements: Sepal Length, Sepal Width, Petal Length, Petal Width. Petal features are most important for classification.

🌼 SETOSA • 1.0-1.9 cm • FAR LEFTSmall petals • 100% isolated • No overlap • Easiest to classify • 100% accuracy
🌸 VERSICOLOR • 3.0-5.1 cm • MIDDLEMedium petals • Slight overlap with Virginica • Needs AI to differentiate
🌺 VIRGINICA • 4.5-6.9 cm • FAR TOPLarge petals • Biggest flowers • Top cluster • 94% accuracy
💡 WHY FAR SEPARATION MATTERS?Far clusters = High accuracy. If dots mixed, AI confuses. Here 96.7% because dots are separate!
